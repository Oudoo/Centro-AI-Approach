Replacing the Recruiter Pipeline with an
Agentic, AI-Driven Hiring Engine

This briefing maps Centro CDX from a manually-orchestrated, recruiter-led BPO hiring funnel to a multi-agent, cloud-native talent engine spanning sourcing → screening → assessment → live virtual interview → offer. The plan layers best-of-breed SaaS evaluation tools on top of our existing AWS, Azure and Genesys footprint — not a forklift replacement of the HRIS.

Bottom Line

Adopt a two-vendor "Brain + Hands" stack: TestGorilla for objective scoring + Paradox (Olivia) for conversational scheduling and follow-up — orchestrated by AWS Bedrock + Lambda with Genesys Cloud CX handling the voice interview leg. Layer in Talview only where proctored technical or executive video is required. Estimated payback ≤ 9 months on Centro's MENA hiring volume.

01

Strategic Context — Why Now

Three forces are converging that make the next 12 months the right window for Centro CDX to industrialise its hiring.

1 · BPO growth outpaces TA capacity

Centro's multinational BPO footprint adds net new agent reqs every quarter. Linear TA team scaling pushes cost-per-hire up and pushes time-to-hire past competitive thresholds — candidates accept the first offer that reaches them, usually within 5–7 days.

2 · MENA candidate channels are mobile-first

In Egypt and the UAE, WhatsApp opens at ≥95% while recruitment-email opens hover near 18%. Any pipeline that leans on email will keep losing candidates to faster competitors. Arabic-aware NLP is now table-stakes, not a wishlist item.

3 · LLM cost curve crossed the threshold

Hosted frontier-class models on AWS Bedrock and Azure OpenAI now make per-candidate AI cost a rounding error. Two years ago a CV-scoring agent would have been a R&D bet; today it is a procurement decision with a 6–9 month payback window.

North-Star Outcome

A candidate applies on Tuesday at 14:00 → receives a WhatsApp greeting in < 60 seconds → completes a 12-min skill assessment by 14:30 → is auto-booked for a Wednesday-morning live video panel with the hiring manager → receives a verbal offer by Friday. Zero recruiter touches for the 80% volume tier; recruiters focus exclusively on the strategic 20%.

Leak 1 · CV Screen

~60% of CVs are reviewed > 48h after submission. Top-quartile candidates exit the funnel before being contacted.

Leak 2 · Phone Screen

Phone tag, voicemail rounds, and "I'll call you back" loops add 2–4 days. Candidates in MENA strongly prefer WhatsApp text-first contact.

Leak 3 · Scheduling

Hiring-manager calendars are the single biggest constraint. No-shows on first-round panels run 15–25% without active nudges.

Leak 4 · Offer Handoff

Background check, contract draft, signing, and HRIS provisioning live in different tools. Each handoff loses 12–36 hours.

Sample candidate trace · BPO agent role
  1. Candidate clicks WhatsApp link on LinkedIn ad.
  2. Paradox greets in Arabic + English, qualifies basics (shifts, language, location).
  3. Bedrock-hosted Claude reads CV from S3 → returns structured fit score in < 2 s.
  4. If fit ≥ 70, Paradox sends TestGorilla link via WhatsApp.
  5. Candidate finishes 12-min assessment on mobile → webhook fires.
  6. If score ≥ 75, Paradox books Wednesday 10:00 panel on hiring-mgr Outlook.
  7. Genesys Architect dials the panel 5 min before — Agent Copilot opens.
  8. Decision → Zoho People auto-creates candidate record + sends offer letter template.
  9. All eight steps logged to immutable audit lake.
Why this composition wins
  • ✓No vendor lock — each tier swappable. If Paradox raises prices, swap with Phenom or Mya.
  • ✓Reuses Centro infra — Zoho, AWS, Azure, Genesys already in production.
  • ✓Data residency by design — sensitive PII never leaves Centro VPCs.
  • ✓Audit-first — every AI score is explainable and reproducible.
  • ✓Recruiter-augmenting — TAs supervise the 20% exception flow, not the 80% routine flow.

CTO Decision Framework

Five gating questions. A "no" on any one of them is a halt-and-fix signal — not a kill signal — but the answer must be on record before procurement.

Q1

Do we have an executive sponsor who owns the people-side change?

Owner = CHRO + CTO joint sign-off. Without it, recruiters revert to spreadsheets within 90 days.

Q2

Is the baseline (TTH, CPH, NPS) measurable today?

If we cannot measure today, we cannot prove improvement tomorrow. Phase 0 fixes this.

Q3

Has Legal blessed the AI-disclosure copy for EU + GCC candidates?

EU AI Act Art 26 + GDPR Art 22 + UAE PDPL all require active candidate notice + opt-out path.

Q4

Is there a single integration owner inside Engineering?

Lambda + EventBridge + Zoho hooks need one named accountable engineer. No "shared ownership".

Q5

Will the recruiter incentive plan shift to reward AI-augmented behaviour?

If TAs are still measured on raw fills, they will treat AI as adversary, not co-pilot. HR Ops must move to "fills × candidate NPS × 6-mo retention". This is the single most under-funded change-management work-stream in every AI hiring rollout I have seen.

Recommendation to the CTO

Approve Phase 0 + Phase 1 today. Sign RFPs with Paradox and TestGorilla. Stand up the AWS Lambda glue layer in parallel. Defer Talview and Sapia decisions to Phase 2 gate review. Defer Eightfold to year-2 strategic planning.

Hold a 30-min monthly steering with CHRO, Head of TA, and Engineering Director. First go-live event: TestGorilla pilot on two BPO accounts by Day 75. First measurable outcome: Phase 1 gate report on Day 90 (screen accuracy, NPS, recruiter capacity).
