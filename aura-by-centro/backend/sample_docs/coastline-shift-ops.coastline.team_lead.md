## Shift bidding
Coastline runs a weekly shift-bid window that opens Thursday 10:00 and closes
Friday 16:00. Bids are awarded by seniority and adherence score.

## Escalation matrix
Tier 1 issues route to the floor team lead. Tier 2 (SLA breach risk) escalates
to the Coastline operations manager. Tier 3 (client-impacting outage) pages the
account director immediately.

## Overtime authorization
Overtime for Coastline must be pre-authorized by a team lead and logged against
the correct project code before the shift begins.
