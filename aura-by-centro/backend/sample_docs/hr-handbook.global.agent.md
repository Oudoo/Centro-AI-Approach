# Centro HR Handbook (Excerpt)

## Working hours and attendance
Standard shifts are 8 hours plus a 1-hour unpaid break. Clock in and out via
Zoho People > Attendance. Three unexcused late arrivals in a month trigger a
review with your team lead.

## Remote and hybrid work
Hybrid eligibility depends on your account and role. Submit a hybrid request in
Zoho People > Self Service. Approval is at your reporting manager's discretion
and is reviewed quarterly.

## Code of conduct
All employees are expected to uphold Centro's values: Innovation, Efficiency,
Operational Excellence, Accountability, and Precision. Harassment, data misuse,
and sharing client information outside your authorized account scope are grounds
for disciplinary action.

## Expenses and reimbursement
Submit expense claims within 30 days with receipts attached in Zoho Expense.
Pre-approval is required for any single item over USD 100.
