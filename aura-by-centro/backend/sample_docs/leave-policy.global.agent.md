# Centro Leave Policy (Excerpt)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue annual leave based on tenure and contract. Your live
balance is shown in Zoho People > Leave Tracker before you submit a request.
Carry-over of unused days is capped and expires at the end of Q1 of the
following year.

## Sick leave
Notify your team lead before your shift start time. For absences longer than two
consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to Zoho People.

## Public holidays
The official holiday calendar is localized per account and region. Find it in
Zoho People > Leave Tracker > Holidays and filter by your location.

## How to submit resignation
Open Zoho People > Self Service > Separation, enter your notice period and last
working day, and submit. Your reporting manager and HR are notified
automatically. Standard notice is 30 days unless your contract states otherwise.
