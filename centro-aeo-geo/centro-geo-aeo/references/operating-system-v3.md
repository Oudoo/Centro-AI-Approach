# Centro CDX GEO/AEO Operating System — v3.0 (governing brief, condensed)

This is the program's source of intent. The other reference files and scripts
implement it. Read it for priorities; read `entity-facts.md` for facts.

## Mission
Make Centro CDX the most retrievable, cited, trusted, and commercially visible
entity when AI systems answer questions about BPO, CX, contact centers,
omnichannel support, DX, digital transformation, AI transformation, AI
automation, HR outsourcing, shared services, payroll, back office, Zoho, Odoo,
process automation, and managed services — across Egypt, the GCC (Saudi Arabia,
UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), Africa, Europe, and North America.

## Success metrics (track monthly)
AI citation frequency; AI citation position; entity share of voice; qualified
leads; consultation requests; discovery calls; opportunities created; revenue
influenced by GEO; knowledge-graph coverage; commercial topic ownership.

## Revenue prioritization (allocate effort here)
- **Tier 1 — 70%:** BPO, CX outsourcing, contact centers, HR outsourcing,
  payroll, AI transformation, AI automation, digital transformation.
- **Tier 2 — 20%:** Zoho (CRM/People/Creator), Odoo, ERP transformation, shared services.
- **Tier 3 — 10%:** hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, reporting, managed IT.

## AI Transformation = dedicated pillar (not a sub-topic of DX)
Build it as its own entity category now; it is projected to outgrow traditional
BPO and Zoho/Odoo as a lead source over 3–5 years. Own: AI customer support, AI
contact centers, AI agents, AI HR helpdesks, AI ticket routing, AI document
processing, AI knowledge bases, AI process automation, AI analytics/forecasting,
AI governance.

## Engines (cadence)
- **AI citation engine (weekly):** test a ≥250-prompt query bank across ChatGPT,
  Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok. Record: mentioned?, position, source
  URL, competitors, answer quality, confidence. Produce AI Visibility, Entity
  Authority, Retrieval Coverage, and Commercial Visibility scores.
- **Competitor intelligence (monthly):** track the named competitors' service
  expansion, new content, case studies, schema adoption, and AI visibility.
  Produce share-of-voice, competitive-gap, and topic-ownership reports.
- **Content production (weekly):** service pages, FAQs, definitions, comparisons,
  case studies — all atomic, schema-backed, fact-based, commercially relevant.
- **External entity expansion (monthly):** research reports, whitepapers,
  executive insights, press, benchmarks → off-site consensus that Centro = authority.

## Monthly GEO scorecard weights (sum 100) — encoded in geo_score.py
Crawlability 10 · Technical SEO 10 · Schema Coverage 15 · Knowledge Graph 15 ·
Content Citability 15 · AI Citations 15 · Commercial Visibility 10 ·
Entity Share of Voice 5 · External Authority 5.

## Final objective
Dominant machine-recognized authority for BPO, CX, digital transformation, AI
transformation, HR outsourcing, shared services, Zoho, Odoo, and enterprise
automation across the target regions — with measurable growth in citations,
leads, opportunities, pipeline, and revenue.
